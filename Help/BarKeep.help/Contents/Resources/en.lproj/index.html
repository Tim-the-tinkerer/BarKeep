BarKeep Help

Tidy your Mac menu bar. Hide seldom-used app icons, keep system icons (Wi‑Fi, Clock, and friends) where you need them, and toggle with a click or keyboard shortcut.

Overview

BarKeep is a menu-bar-only macOS app (no Dock icon). It places two controls in the status item area:

  • │ — the hide boundary (divider)
  • BK — the control chevron you click to collapse or expand

When you collapse, icons to the left of │ are pushed off-screen. Icons to the right of BK stay visible. No Accessibility or Screen Recording permission is required.

Getting Started

  1. Launch BarKeep (or run ./build-app.sh from the project folder).
  2. Find │ and BK in the menu bar (usually left of Wi‑Fi / Clock).
  3. Hold ⌘ and drag app icons you want hidden fully to the left of │.
  4. Keep system icons (Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth, Sound, Clock, Control Center) to the right of BK.
  5. Click BK once to hide; click again to show.
Dropping an icon onto the letters “BK” does not hide it. Only icons left of the thin │ mark are in the hide zone.

Menu Bar Zones

Think of the bar in three parts (left → right toward the clock):

[ hide these apps ] │ [ keepers ] BK [ Wi‑Fi · Bluetooth · Clock ]
ZoneWhat goes hereWhen collapsed
Left of │ App icons you rarely need Hidden
Between │ and BK App icons you still want visible Stay visible
Right of BK System modules and the clock Stay visible

Hide & Show

  • Click BK — toggle collapsed / expanded
  • ⌃⌘B — same toggle from the keyboard (if enabled in Settings)
  • Right-click BK — open the control menu

BarKeep begins expanded so a previous session cannot leave you without a chevron. If Start collapsed is on in Settings, it collapses about a second after launch once icons have settled.

Under the hood, collapse expands the divider’s length (the same public-API technique used by Hidden Bar and Ice) so items ordered left of │ leave the visible bar.

Settings

Open Settings from the right-click menu on BK, or with ⌘, while a BarKeep window is key.

OptionDefaultDescription
Start collapsed Off After launch, wait about a second for icons to settle, then collapse.
Auto-hide after delay Off After you expand, collapse again after 5–60 seconds.
Show on hover Off Expand when the pointer is in the right half of the menu bar.
Launch at login Off Start BarKeep when you log in (macOS Login Items).
Global hotkey ⌃⌘B On Toggle hide/show from the keyboard.

Multiple Displays

Status icons can appear on more than one menu bar. Collapse and expand apply to the bar where you click BK. System icons on another display are not treated as “left of │” on the display you are using.

Tips

  • If │ and BK sit too far right (between Wi‑Fi and Clock), use Reset BarKeep Position… or ⌘-drag them left of the system cluster.
  • Your arrangement of other apps’ icons is remembered by macOS. BarKeep only seeds its own position once.
  • For a stable “launch at login” setup, keep BarKeep.app in a fixed place (for example /Applications or this project folder).
  • First-run tips can be shown again from the right-click menu: How to Arrange Icons….

Troubleshooting

Icons only shift, they don’t hide

Make sure the icons you want hidden are fully left of │, then click BK again. Rebuild with ./build-app.sh if you are on an older build.

BK or the chevron disappeared

Quit and reopen BarKeep, or run:

pkill -x BarKeep; open ~/BarKeep/BarKeep.app

BarKeep always starts expanded so the control can reappear.

System icons disappeared when I collapsed

⌘-drag │ and BK so Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth, Sound, and Clock sit to the right of BK. Then collapse again. Only app icons should be left of │.

Hotkey does nothing

Open Settings and confirm Global hotkey ⌃⌘B is on. Another app may have registered the same combination.

Help window says help is unavailable

Rebuild with ./build-app.sh so the help book is copied into BarKeep.app/Contents/Resources/.
